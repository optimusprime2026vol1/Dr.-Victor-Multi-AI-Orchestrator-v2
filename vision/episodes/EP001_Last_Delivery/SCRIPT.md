# EP001 — LAST DELIVERY
**Format:** Short film · ~10 min  
**Language:** Hinglish (natural spoken)  
**Status:** SCRIPT DRAFT — awaiting Founder check  
**Isolation:** Vision / YouTube only — not AURA2

---

## LOGLINE
Ek delivery boy ki aakhri raat ki order me illegal maal nahi, ek parivaar ki sachchai ka proof nikalta hai — aur usse choose karna padta hai: paise ya iman.

---

## SCENE 1 — BIKE / NIGHT ROAD (0:00–1:00)
**Visual:** City night, neon, Rahul bike pe, bag peeche.

**RAHUL (phone, earphone)**  
Haan Ammi… aaj late hounga. Last order hai. Kal subah medicine ka paisa rakh dunga.

**AMMI (phone, soft)**  
Thak mat jaana. Khana khaya?

**RAHUL**  
Haan. Bas yeh order drop karke aata hoon.

*(He checks app: “MRS SHARMA — FRAGILE — Priority”)*

**RAHUL (to himself)**  
Fragile… theek hai. Seedha drop, seedha ghar.

---

## SCENE 2 — SOCIETY GATE (1:00–2:30)
**Visual:** Gate light, security napping, Rahul parks.

Rahul box nikalta hai. Label clear: MRS SHARMA, Ground Floor, B-2.

Ek car slow hoti hai. **VIKRAM** window se:

**VIKRAM**  
Bhai, Sharma ji ka parcel?

**RAHUL**  
Aap…?

**VIKRAM**  
Relative. Main le longa. Unhe disturb mat karo, tabiyat theek nahi.

*(Vikram wallet nikalta hai — notes)*

**VIKRAM**  
Tumhara tip. Double. Bas yahi de do.

**RAHUL**  
Sir, app pe customer signature chahiye. Main khud deliver karta hoon.

**VIKRAM** *(smile, thoda tight)*  
Samajhdar bano. Raat hai.

Rahul gate ki taraf chal padta hai. Vikram car me baitha rehta hai — dekhte hue.

---

## SCENE 3 — MRS SHARMA’S DOOR (2:30–5:30)
**Visual:** Dim corridor, doorbell.

**MRS SHARMA** *(opens, cautious)*  
Haan?

**RAHUL**  
Delivery ma’am. Mrs Sharma? Fragile.

Woh box dekhti hai — saans rukti hai.

**MRS SHARMA**  
Andar aao.

Ghar simple. Photo wall pe — young man (son). Rahul box table pe rakhta hai.

**RAHUL**  
Signature yahan…

**MRS SHARMA**  
Ruko.

Woh box kholti nahi — sirf tape ke paas haath rakhiti hai.

**MRS SHARMA**  
Tumne kisi ko bataya yeh kahan aa raha hai?

**RAHUL**  
Nahi. Bas app.

**MRS SHARMA**  
Bahar koi tha?

Rahul sochta hai — Vikram.

**RAHUL**  
Ek aadmi… bola relative hai. Parcel le lena chahta tha.

Mrs Sharma ka chehra badal jata hai.

**MRS SHARMA**  
Woh relative nahi. Woh… usi ka kaaran hai ki mera beta ab yahan nahi.

*(Silence)*

**MRS SHARMA**  
Is box me paise nahi. Dawai nahi. Isme uski will hai… aur proof. Jo log dabaana chahte hain.

**RAHUL**  
Ma’am main sirf delivery…

**MRS SHARMA**  
Tumne already choose kar liya tha jab tumne usse parcel nahi diya.

Doorbell. Ya door pe dhakka — soft but clear.

**VIKRAM (outside)**  
Aunty? Main hoon. Parcel aa gaya na?

Mrs Sharma Rahul ki taraf dekhti hai.

**MRS SHARMA** *(whisper)*  
Back door. Jaldi.

---

## SCENE 4 — BACK LANE / CHOICE (5:30–8:00)
**Visual:** Narrow lane, Rahul box ke saath, saans tez.

Phone ring — unknown number. Phir Vikram ka message style pressure (or call):

**VIKRAM (voice / call)**  
Bhai, soch lo. Tumhare ghar me bhi family hai. Yeh box tumhare kaam ka nahi.

Rahul rukta hai. Box bhaari lagta hai.

Flash: Ammi ki medicine, rent.

Woh box kholta nahi — sirf label dekhta hai. Phir apna phone:

**RAHUL** *(dial, low)*  
Police… ek delivery hai… mujhe lagta hai isme saboot hai… address…

---

## SCENE 5 — RESOLUTION (8:00–10:00)
**Visual:** Police light soft distance OR Mrs Sharma door pe again — cleaner resolution without heavy violence.

**Option A (preferred, cleaner):**  
Rahul wapas Mrs Sharma ke paas nahi — woh box authorized person / police help se safe process.  
Short beat: morning, Rahul bike pe, Ammi ko call.

**RAHUL**  
Medicine ka paisa aa jayega Ammi. Aaj thoda late tha… lekin theek kiya.

**AMMI**  
Bas tu theek hai na?

**RAHUL**  
Haan.

*(He rides. Bag halka. One beat of relief — not hero speech.)*

**Title card:**  
LAST DELIVERY  
*Kabhi-kabhi sahi order woh hota hai jo app pe nahi dikhta.*
