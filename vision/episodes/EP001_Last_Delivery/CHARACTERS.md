# EP001 — Last Delivery
## Character Bible (AI-generated after Founder approved Topic C)

**Tone:** Crime / moral thriller · grounded · Hindi-Hinglish dialogue friendly  
**Target length:** 8–12 minutes  
**Platform:** Separate YouTube channel only (not AURA2 / not Instagram)

---

### 1. RAHUL (Lead)
- **Age:** 24
- **Look:** Thin, tired eyes, simple T-shirt, delivery bag always on shoulder
- **Job:** Night-shift delivery rider (bike)
- **Want:** Is month ka rent + ammi ki medicine; “bas ek aur order”
- **Fear:** Job lose karna; galti se galat cheez deliver karna
- **Arc:** Order complete karna → truth dekhna → choose karna (easy money vs right thing)

### 2. MRS. SHARMA (Customer / key)
- **Age:** 58
- **Look:** Simple saree, ground-floor flat, soft voice
- **Want:** Apne bete ka “last thing” safe rakhna
- **Secret:** Parcel me illegal maal nahi — bete ki handwritten will + proof jo family dispute solve kar sakta hai
- **Arc:** Stranger (Rahul) pe trust vs family se darr

### 3. VIKRAM (Antagonist pressure)
- **Age:** 32
- **Look:** Smart casual, calm face, threat in politeness
- **Role:** Relative / “well-wisher” jo proof dabaana chahta hai
- **Want:** Parcel usse pehle mil jaye
- **Method:** Money offer → pressure → follow

### 4. AMMI (Rahul’s mother) — brief presence
- **Age:** 50s
- **Role:** Phone call / motivation only (screen time short)
- **Function:** Rahul ko “sahi kaam” yaad dilana without lecture
