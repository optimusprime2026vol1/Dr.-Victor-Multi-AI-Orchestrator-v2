## How to use
1. Open [Gemini](https://gemini.google.com) (or Google AI Studio)
2. Paste **one prompt at a time**
3. Pick best image → download
4. Character sheet ko reference / “same person” ke liye baad ke prompts me describe karo

Style lock (har prompt me same feel):
`cinematic still, Indian urban night drama, photorealistic, natural skin, shallow depth of field, film grain subtle, 35mm look, no text overlay, no watermark, no logo`

## Tips for Gemini
- Agar face change ho jaye: prompt me add karo `same face as previous reference, identical person`
- Box har baar: `brown box red tape FRAGILE`
- Jacket: `blue delivery jacket` hamesha
- Avoid: blood, weapons, graphic crime — story pressure-based hai
