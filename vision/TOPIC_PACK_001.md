# VISION — Topic Pack 001 (Founder Approval Required)

**Date:** 2026-08-21  
**Rule:** Pick ONE → then AI builds characters + full script. No production without approval.

Research basis: India micro-drama / short Hindi drama boom (YouTube + short-form), strong demand for emotional family drama, romance conflict, and psychological thriller shorts.

---

## Option A — *Galat Number*
- **Genre:** Thriller / suspense  
- **Premise:** Ek ladki ko raat ko unknown number se call aata hai — caller uske ghar ki details batata hai jo sirf family jaanti hai.  
- **Why it can work:** Hook pehle 30 sec me; mystery + fear; short film friendly.  
- **Risk:** Similar “mystery call” tropes common — twist strong hona zaroori.  
- **Suggested length:** 8–12 min  

## Option B — *Doosri Maa*
- **Genre:** Emotional family drama  
- **Premise:** Stepmother aur beti ka tension; ek secret letter se pata chalta hai asli sacrifice kisne kiya.  
- **Why it can work:** Family + emotion India me strong retention; female audience micro-drama me heavy.  
- **Risk:** Melodrama overdo ho sakta hai — simple, honest tone rakhna.  
- **Suggested length:** 10–15 min  

## Option C — *Last Delivery*
- **Genre:** Crime / moral thriller  
- **Premise:** Delivery boy aakhri parcel leke jaata hai; box me illegal cheez nahi, ek insaan ki zindagi badalne wala proof hai.  
- **Why it can work:** Working-class hero + one-night tension; clear visual locations (street, society gate).  
- **Risk:** Ending moral lecture jaisa na lage.  
- **Suggested length:** 8–12 min  

## Option D — *Seen at 2:13*
- **Genre:** Psychological / relationship  
- **Premise:** Girlfriend ke phone pe “seen” tick; us raat uska location kisi aur ke message se match karta hai.  
- **Why it can work:** Modern trust issue; young audience; tight closed-room + phone UI storytelling.  
- **Risk:** Misogyny / one-sided blame avoid karna.  
- **Suggested length:** 8–10 min  

## Option E — *Bus 42*
- **Genre:** Social drama / twist  
- **Premise:** Roz ki bus me do strangers; ek din bus rukti nahi — aur unme se ek jaanta hai kyun.  
- **Why it can work:** Single location pressure; slow dread; memorable ending image.  
- **Risk:** Too abstract without strong character want.  
- **Suggested length:** 8–12 min
