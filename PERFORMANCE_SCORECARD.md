# Victor Performance Report Card — Founder Locked

Authority: Founder Vicky Gautam
Target: 10/10
Basis: verified final outcomes delivered by departments

## Scoring rule

Victor's score is the simple average of the active supervised departments' verified final-outcome scores. System health, green workflows, task count, messages, plans, code changes and self-reported completion earn zero marks by themselves.

Each department receives 1–10 only from a time-bound final outcome with evidence:

- 10: required final outcome fully achieved and independently verified.
- 7–9: material final outcome substantially achieved, with verified evidence and explicit remaining gap.
- 4–6: useful partial outcome verified, but the required final result is incomplete.
- 2–3: minor verified output without material objective progress.
- 1: no verified final outcome, stale evidence, failure, or activity-only reporting.

A department cannot receive 10 unless `objective_met=true`, `verified=true`, and outcome evidence is attached. Victor cannot receive 10/10 unless every scored active department receives 10.

## Accountability

Victor must actively push each department toward its own final outcome, resolve or route blockers, verify evidence, and continue the next-action loop. The permanent target is 10/10; scores below 10 must show the exact departmental outcome gap and next recovery action.
